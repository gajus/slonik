fix: `sql.and` and `sql.or` wrap each member and the combined expression in parentheses so that nested and embedded conditions keep their meaning, e.g. `sql.and([sql.or([a, b]), c])` now produces `(((a) OR (b)) AND (c))` instead of `a OR b AND c`. This changes the SQL generated by every `sql.and` / `sql.or` call.
